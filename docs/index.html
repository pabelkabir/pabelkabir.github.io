Research identity

Molecular behavior is shaped by environment.

Our work asks how electrostatics, hydrogen bonding, solvation, conformational sampling, and molecular architecture reshape excited states and intermolecular interactions. We develop reproducible computational workflows and apply them to flavins and fluorescent proteins, photoactive molecules, molecular association, and functional ionic materials.

How do local electrostatics, hydrogen bonding, redox and protonation state, protein structure, and molecular architecture control absorption, fluorescence, intersystem crossing, and charge-transfer pathways?
